My Story

Telling stories through the lens

I'm Goutham Krishna K S, a creative media professional from Thrissur, Kerala, currently pursuing a Bachelor of Arts in Journalism & Mass Communication at Lovely Professional University, Phagwara.

My interest in filmmaking and visual storytelling has led me to work across video production, photography and content creation from directing and editing a short film to operating cameras and editing content for podcasts and university media events.

I enjoy the full creative process, from concept development and scripting to shooting and post-production, and I'm always looking for the next story worth telling through video, photography or social content.
